About

The PIZZA Project

PIZZA is a community-powered NFT crowdfund built on Citrea — the first rollup that brings programmable smart contracts to Bitcoin. We believe the best things in life are shared: a warm slice, a good community, and ownership of something meaningful on-chain.

Each pizza slice is a unique, generative pixel art NFT with randomized toppings, crusts, and flavor traits. The collection is funded through a transparent crowdfunding mechanism: if we hit the goal, everyone gets their slice. If not, everyone gets a full refund.

How It Works

  • Connect any EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase, Trust, OKX, etc.)
  • The site automatically switches your wallet to the Citrea mainnet
  • Choose how many slices you want and click Mint
  • Your cBTC is held in the crowdfund smart contract
  • If the goal is reached, NFTs are distributed to all patrons
  • If the goal is NOT reached, you receive a full refund in cBTC

Why Citrea?

Citrea is the first EVM-compatible rollup on Bitcoin, settling directly on the Bitcoin L1. By building on Citrea, PIZZA inherits Bitcoin's security while enabling the expressive smart contracts needed for NFTs, crowdfunding, and DeFi. Your slices are secured by Bitcoin.

Collection Details

  • Supply: 1,000 unique pizza slices
  • Network: Citrea Mainnet (Chain ID 4114)
  • Currency: cBTC (Citrea Bitcoin)
  • Standard: ERC-721
  • Art: Fully on-chain, generative pixel art
  • Refund policy: 100% refund if goal not met
